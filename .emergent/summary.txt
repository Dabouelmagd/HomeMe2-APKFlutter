**For Residents:** Family account management, direct communication, payment & transparency, important notifications, booking services, utility bill management. Residents can also engage in multimedia chats, voice messages, and message search.
**General:** Mock payment system, file upload, in-app and real-time (WebSocket & Push) notifications, basic authentication, trilingual support (English, Arabic, French), PWA capability for mobile installation (in progress), File Organization/Gallery (backend implemented), Message Scheduling (backend implemented).
